## Feature 1 — Listing Optimisation

Your product listing is the first thing a customer sees. A weak title, vague bullet points, or missing keywords means you lose the sale before the customer even clicks. Our AI researches your top competitors and rewrites your listing based on what is actually working in your category.

### Competitor Research & Summary

We look at the top-ranking listings in your category and build a clear summary of what they are saying - the exact keywords, phrases, and selling points they use. You see this in plain language, not raw data.

- Top 10 competitor listings analysed automatically
- Common keywords and phrases highlighted
- Gaps identified - things you are missing that competitors include
- Updated regularly so you stay current

### AI-Generated Title, Bullets & Description

Based on the competitor research, our AI writes a new title, bullet points, and product description for your listing - optimised for both Amazon search and real customers reading it.

- Title crafted to include high-traffic keywords naturally
- Bullet points rewritten to highlight benefits, not just features
- Description written in a clear, persuasive style
- You review and approve before anything goes live

### Image Optimiser

Upload your product image and our engine significantly enhances it - better lighting, cleaner background, sharper detail, and a more professional finish. No photographer or designer needed.

- One-click image enhancement powered by our product engine
- Background removal and replacement with clean, Amazon-compliant backdrops
- Brightness, contrast, and sharpness automatically improved
- Output is ready to upload directly to your listing

### Suppressed & Underperforming Listing Detection

- Listings that are suppressed on Amazon flagged immediately
- Low-converting listings identified based on impressions vs. clicks
- Specific reason given for each underperforming listing

## Feature 2 — Advertising (PPC) Performance

Most businesses are either overspending on the wrong keywords or have campaigns that have not been reviewed in months. Our AI reads your ad data daily and tells you where money is being wasted and where to invest more.2

**Problems we catch:**
- Keywords that cost money but never convert
- Broad match terms attracting irrelevant clicks
- Campaigns with bids that are too high or too low
- Search terms converting organically that you are also paying for

**What we recommend:**
- Specific keywords to pause or reduce bids on
- New keyword opportunities based on your search term report
- Bid adjustment suggestions with expected impact
- Negative keyword additions to stop wasted spend
